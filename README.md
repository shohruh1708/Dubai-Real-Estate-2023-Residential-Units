# Dubai-Real-Estate-2023-Residential-Units
End-to-end data analysis of Dubai real estate market using Python, SQL, and Power BI. Explores pricing trends, demand patterns, and investment insights including median vs average gap and high-demand areas.

The Dubai real estate market is predominantly driven by mid-range properties, with approximately 49% of transactions in the mid segment, compared to 41% in the affordable segment and only 9.5% in the luxury category. This indicates that around 90% of all transactions occur outside the luxury market, making mid-range properties the most stable and demand-driven segment.

In terms of liquidity, areas such as Jumeirah Village Circle (JVC) account for about 14.5% of total transactions, followed by Business Bay at 8.9% and Dubai Marina at 4.7%. These locations show the highest transaction activity, making them ideal for investors seeking quicker resale opportunities.

The average property price in Dubai stands at approximately 2.02 million AED; however, this figure is significantly skewed by high-end luxury transactions. The median price is much lower at around 1.35 million AED—about 33% less—making it a more accurate representation of the typical market value.

Additionally, there are extreme outliers in the dataset, with 32 properties priced at more than 50 times the average and a maximum price reaching nearly 500 million AED (~136 million USD). These ultra-luxury properties, often located in areas like Palm Jumeirah, should be analyzed separately to avoid distortion in overall market insights.
